Title: Legacy
Date: 2026-09-29
Show: full

Who decides what actions live on in history?

Leaves may never see the light they need.
Still, nutrients get sent their way,
As if hope for light is never lost.

Some fight for their light,
Not caring what it costs others.
Some reach the light,
But get overtaken.

Brief moments of light,
Earned or given,
Is how history is made.
